Services

Two things I'm known for — and what else I do

Each engagement begins with a discovery conversation. From there, we define the right structure, whether a focused strategic project, an ongoing advisory relationship, or a workshop or testbed initiative.

What I'm known for

Geospatially Aware AI Agents

Strategy and design for AI agents that understand location, context, and the physical world around them — from early technology assessment through pilot and production.

We advise organizations building AI agents, wearables, and spatial products that need to reason about real-world location and context — not just process text or images in isolation.

This work draws directly on hands-on platform experience building MyGeoVerse, and on standards leadership as Chief Adoption Officer of the OGC's GeoPose working group — the standard that defines how location and orientation are represented across spatial computing platforms.

Our work can include technology and platform assessments, product strategy for geospatially aware agents, integration planning with GeoPose and related open standards, and pilot design and evaluation.

Serves: product and innovation leaders building AI agents, wearables, or spatial products
Typical engagement: technology assessment, fixed-scope project, or ongoing advisory

What I'm known for

Persistent & Shared XR Experiences

Design and advisory for multi-user, persistent XR — spatial experiences that stay put, stay in sync across users, and stay interoperable across devices and platforms.

We help organizations — tourism, retail, education, smart-venue operators, cities — design persistent, multi-user XR experiences that are built correctly the first time, rather than locked into a single vendor's proprietary stack.

This work is grounded in the open protocols developed through Open AR Cloud, where we serve as Executive Co-Director, and the Metaverse Standards Forum, keeping experiences interoperable as platforms and devices evolve.

Our work can include spatial experience design, multi-user architecture planning, interoperability and open-standards guidance, and testbed design and implementation.

Serves: tourism, retail, education, smart-venue operators, and cities building persistent XR experiences
Typical engagement: fixed-scope project, testbed engagement, or ongoing advisory

Academy Programs
