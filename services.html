Five ways to work with Hantal Advising

Each engagement begins with a discovery conversation. From there, we define the right structure, whether a focused strategic project, an ongoing advisory relationship, a workshop or testbed initiative, or hands-on representation for U.S. market entry and international growth.

Signature offering

AI & Spatial Computing Strategy

We help organizations understand and act on the convergence of AI, spatial computing, AI glasses, and location-aware technologies.

We advise companies, universities, and technology organizations on how emerging AI and spatial technologies may affect their products, services, operations, and long-term strategy.

Our work can include technology landscape assessments, product and platform strategy, AI and XR adoption planning, spatially aware AI, AI glasses and audio-first interfaces, geospatial technologies, interoperability, and emerging standards.

The goal is not to chase hype, but to identify where these technologies can create practical value and where organizations should invest, experiment, partner, or wait.

Serves: companies, universities, and technology organizations evaluating AI and spatial computing
Typical engagement: technology assessment, fixed-scope project, or ongoing retainer

EdTech & Education Innovation

We help universities, education institutions, and EdTech companies develop innovative programs, partnerships, and technology-enabled learning models.

We work with universities, schools, training organizations, technology centers, and EdTech companies that want to expand their capabilities, modernize learning experiences, or build international programs.

Areas of support can include AI in education, immersive learning, XR-based training, simulation, international partnerships, industry-academic collaboration, workforce development, entrepreneurship programs, and new educational technology initiatives.

For EdTech companies, we can also support market positioning, institutional partnerships, go-to-market strategy, and expansion into the U.S. or international markets.

For education institutions, we help connect academic objectives with practical technology, industry partnerships, and sustainable program models.

Academy Programs

We also help education institutions launch hands-on Academy programs that prepare both educators and students for the spatial computing and spatially aware AI era.

Educator & Train-the-Trainer Programs
Designed for faculty, teachers, instructors, and institutional technology leaders who want to understand spatial computing and bring it into their own classrooms and programs. Training can cover spatial intelligence, localization, open standards, geospatial content creation, AR navigation, and location-aware AI, with pathways toward instructor certification.

Student & Creator Programs
Designed for students, young innovators, technologists, and creatives who want practical experience building with XR, geospatial technologies, AI-assisted development, and location-aware AI. Advanced tracks can culminate in a working prototype, portfolio project, or testbed deployment.

Academy programs can also be integrated with institutional testbeds for teaching, experimentation, research, and real-world deployment.

Serves: universities, education institutions, technology centers, training organizations, and EdTech companies
