The builder

Ali has been a serial entrepreneur since 1999. He co-founded Mezun.com, named the fastest-growing company in the Baltimore–Washington, D.C. area in 2004, and built a track record in international education long before "EdTech" was a category.

Today he is founder & CEO of XR Masters, an operating company in spatial computing behind MyGeoVerse — proof that the strategic advice on offer here comes from someone who has shipped, not just advised.

Standards & interoperability

The standards leader

Very few advisors can claim standards-body leadership. Ali is Executive Co-Director of the Open AR Cloud Association, Chief Adoption Officer of the GeoPose Standards Working Group at the Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC), and Leader of the Forum Labs Initiative at the Metaverse Standards Forum.

This work matters beyond the credential: it means advice grounded in open, interoperable choices — not bets on any single vendor's walled garden. Notable engagements include the GeoPose Summit 2025 in Denver and a W3C interoperability demonstration hosted at Google's campuses in March 2025.

Origins

The person

Ali's path started as a philosophy student at Boğaziçi University, reading Descartes under Arda Denkel — a formative apprenticeship in rigorous, first-principles thinking that still shapes how he takes apart a strategic problem today.

He also brings a 30-year meditation practice and a breathwork certification to the way he shows up in high-stakes rooms — presence and long-horizon thinking as a quiet complement to the commercial work. Those curious about that fuller dimension can find it at atbliss.org.
