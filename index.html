Signature offering

AI & Spatial Computing Strategy

AI is becoming increasingly aware of the physical world around us. At the same time, AI glasses, audio-first interfaces, spatial computing, and geospatial technologies are creating new ways for people to interact with information, places, and digital services.

We help leaders understand what this shift means for their organization and identify where to act. Our advisory work may include:

  • Spatially aware and context-aware AI strategy
  • AI glasses and audio-first interface strategy
  • Spatial computing and XR adoption
  • Geospatial and location-based intelligence
  • Product and platform strategy for emerging interfaces
  • Open standards, interoperability, and spatial web technologies
  • Technology assessments, executive workshops & innovation roadmaps
  • Testbeds, academies, training programs & hands-on tutorials

We help organizations separate meaningful technological shifts from short-term hype and turn emerging capabilities into practical strategy, pilots, testbeds, education programs, products, partnerships, and growth.

Prepare your organization for an AI era that is not only intelligent, but increasingly aware of the world around us.

Founder

Ali C. Hantal

Ali C. Hantal is a serial entrepreneur, technology executive, educator, and international speaker who has been building and advising businesses since 1999.

Having built companies and partnerships across the United States, Europe, and emerging markets, he brings more than two decades of experience connecting technology, business strategy, education, and international growth.

Today, his work focuses increasingly on AI, spatial computing, geospatial technologies, and the emerging spatial web. Through leadership roles in industry and international standards organizations, he is actively involved in shaping how spatially aware AI, AI glasses, and interoperable spatial technologies evolve.

He brings a founder's perspective, an operator's pragmatism, and a strategist's view of what is coming next to every engagement.
