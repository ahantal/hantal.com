Who it's for

AI is moving from understanding information to understanding the physical world. Organizations that operate physical places, such as shopping malls, airports, cruise ship piers, stadiums, museums, cultural heritage sites, tourism destinations, campuses, and other high-traffic venues, will need to decide how their AI understands location, orientation, surroundings, and real-world context.

This includes organizations that haven't started building anything yet, as well as enterprises, public-sector organizations, and technology companies already developing AI, XR, or spatial products. Hantal Advising helps figure out what to build, how to build it, and how to turn it into both a better visitor experience and a business opportunity, grounded in a working platform and the open standards that determine whether these experiences hold up over time.

Founder

Ali C. Hantal

Ali C. Hantal is the founder of XR Masters, a standards lead at OGC and Open AR Cloud, and an advisor on location-aware AI agents and persistent, shared XR experiences. He has been building and advising businesses since 1999.

Having built companies and partnerships across the United States, Europe, Türkiye, and emerging markets, he brings more than two decades of experience connecting technology, business strategy, education, and international growth.

Today, his work focuses on helping organizations bring AI into the physical world. Through leadership roles at the OGC, Open AR Cloud, and the Metaverse Standards Forum, he is actively involved in shaping the open standards these experiences run on.

He brings a founder's perspective, an operator's pragmatism, and a strategist's view of what is coming next to every engagement.
